# Recording the demo video (≤ 2:30)

## Mainnet or testnet?

- **Use mainnet for the core shots.** Judges score "real usage" and "works in Nimiq Pay"; both reference apps
  (Ponte, Zamu) recorded real mainnet flows. A plant needs only **100 NIM**, a feed can be 1–10 NIM, and you can
  withdraw it all afterwards — it is your own stake, nothing is spent except tiny fees.
- **Testnet is fine for rehearsing** and for anything you don't want to wait for on mainnet. Switch Nimiq Pay to
  testnet, open https://sprout-testnet.vercel.app, and get free test NIM from the Nimiq testnet faucet
  (ask in the Nimiq builder community if you can't find the current faucet link). If you show testnet in the video,
  say so on screen.
- **Use `/demo` only for what real time can't show** — evolution after weeks, fast-forward, squad history — and say
  "demo mode" when you do (the app shows a "Demo — no real money" banner anyway).
- Opening the mainnet URL while Nimiq Pay is on testnet (or the other way round) shows the wrong-network screen. That
  is intended, and it's a good 3-second shot for "error handling".
